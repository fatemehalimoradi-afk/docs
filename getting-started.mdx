Install Knit, turn on the app embed, then create a bundle. For Tapcart, headless, and other connected apps, see [Integrations](/integrations).

## Prerequisites

- A Shopify store
- Permission to install apps

## Install

1. Install Knit from the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/knit-bundles).
2. Open **Knit Bundles** from **Apps** in Shopify admin.

Installing creates Knit's automatic discount in Shopify admin → **Discounts**. Its title ends in **(DO NOT DELETE)**. Keep it **Active** — it applies every bundle discount at checkout.

## Turn on the app embed

The app embed is what runs Knit on your online store. Until it is on, no widget appears on product pages.

On the Knit **Home** page, under **Quick setup guide**:

1. Open **Activate app embed**.
2. Click **Open app embeds**.
3. Turn on the **Nameless Bundles** embed and click **Save**.

The step then shows **Status: Active**. You can also do this later from **Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds**. If you save a bundle made from a bundle type while the embed is off, the editor shows an **Enable app embed** button.

## Create a bundle

On **Home**, under **Quick setup guide**, click **Create bundle**. That opens **Choose bundle type**.

- Pick a bundle type card and click **Choose** to fill in a ready form.
- Or click **Build with AI** to describe the bundle to the Bundle Engine.

Follow [Create a bundle](/create-a-bundle), set the status to **Active**, and save.

## Check it on your store

Open a product page the bundle shows on. The widget should appear, and adding the bundle should show the discount in the cart and at checkout.

To see a **Draft** bundle on your store before you activate it, add `?nameless_preview=true` to the product page URL. Previews don't get the checkout discount.
