Install Knit, turn on the app embed, then create a bundle. For Tapcart, headless, and other connected apps, see [Integrations](/integrations).

## Prerequisites

- A Shopify store
- Permission to install apps

## Install

1. Install Knit from the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/knit-bundles).
2. Open **Knit Bundles** from **Apps** in Shopify admin.

## Turn on the app embed

The app embed is what shows Knit on your online store. Until it is on, the widget will not appear on product pages.

On the Knit **Home** page, under **Quick setup guide**:

1. Open **Activate app embed**.
2. Click **Open app embeds**.
3. Enable the Knit embed and save.

You can also do this later from **Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds**.

## Create a bundle

Back on **Home**, under **Quick setup guide**, click **Create bundle**.

That opens the editor. Follow [Create a bundle](/create-a-bundle) to add product sets, conditions, and rewards, then set the bundle to **Active** and save.
