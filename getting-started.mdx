You need a Shopify store and permission to install apps. For Tapcart, headless, and other apps, see [Integrations](/integrations).

## Install

1. Install Fast Bundle 2.0 from the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/fast-bundle-product-bundles).
2. Open **Fast Bundle 2.0** from **Apps** in Shopify admin.

Installing creates the automatic discount **FastBundle (DO NOT DELETE)** in Shopify admin → **Discounts**. Keep it **Active** — it applies every bundle discount at checkout.

## Turn on the app embed

No widget appears until the app embed is on. On **Home**, under **Get started**:

1. Open **Activate the app embed** and click **Activate**.
2. In the theme editor, turn on the **Bundles** embed and click **Save**.

The step then shows **App embed is on**. You can also do this from **Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds**.

## Create a bundle

On **Home**, under **Get started → Create your first bundle**, click **Create bundle**, then either:

- Pick a bundle type card and click **Choose** (and a preset under **Choose a preset**, then **Create bundle**), or
- Click **Build with AI** to describe the bundle to the Bundle Engine.

Follow [Create a bundle](/create-a-bundle), set the status to **Active**, and save.

## Check it on your store

Open a product page the bundle shows on. The widget should appear, and the discount should show in the cart and at checkout.
