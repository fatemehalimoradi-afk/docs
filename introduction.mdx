description: "What Knit Bundles is"
---

Knit Bundles is a Shopify app for selling product bundles with automatic discounts. Shoppers pick the bundle on the product page, and the discount is applied at Shopify checkout.

You create a bundle in two ways:

- **From a bundle type** — pick a ready form such as **Fixed bundle**, fill it in, and Knit builds both the discount logic and the widget.
- **With the Bundle Engine** — describe the bundle to the AI, or build it by hand. You control every piece: the products, the offers and their tiers, the conditions, the rewards, and the widget's code.

Either way, every bundle is the same model underneath: **product sets**, **offers** made of **tiers**, **conditions**, **rewards**, and the **widget**.
