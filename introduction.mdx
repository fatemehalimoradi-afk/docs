description: "What Knit Bundles is"
---

Knit Bundles is a Shopify app for product bundles, volume discounts, and mix-and-match offers. You create the offer in Shopify admin. Both the bundle logic and its appearance are fully customizable, so you can build the exact bundle experience you need. Shoppers see it on the product page, and the discount is applied at Shopify checkout.
