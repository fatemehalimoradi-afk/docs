Every Knit bundle is made of the same pieces — [product sets, offers, tiers, conditions, rewards, and the widget](/bundle-concepts) — and you can combine them in any way the offer needs. The bundle types in the gallery are ready starting points, not limits.

The five bundles below are **examples**, from simple to rich, chosen to show how the pieces combine. Mix their ideas freely: any condition with any reward, several offers in one bundle, triggers, shipping rewards, and a widget that looks however you want.

In each, **Knit handles** the same things: working out which tier applies, every price and saving the widget shows, tagging the cart lines, and applying the discount at Shopify checkout.

## 1. Buy more, save more

**The shopper sees** quantity cards on a product page — "1 item", "2 items, save 10%", "4 items, save 20%" — each showing its price after the discount. Tapping a card sets the quantity.

| Engine piece | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Product set | The product, **The same variant** |
| Offer | One ladder of three tiers |
| Conditions | **Exactly** 1, **At least** 2, **At least** 4 |
| Rewards | None, 10%, 20% **Percentage discount** |

**You customize** the card layout, wording, badges ("Most popular"), and colors.

## 2. A fixed set at one price

**The shopper sees** a "complete the look" card with a shirt, trousers, and a belt, a variant picker for each, and one total: "All three for $99".

| Engine piece | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Product sets | One per product |
| Conditions | **Exactly** 1 from each set, in one tier |
| Reward | **Set price** $99, **Applies per** **Total**, on all three sets |

**You customize** which products, the picture layout, and whether shoppers pick a variant per item.

## 3. Mix and match

**The shopper sees** a grid of products from a collection, a counter "3 of 6 picked", and a price that drops as they add: 3 for $30, 6 for $54.

| Engine piece | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Product set | The collection, **Any mix** |
| Offer | Two tiers: **Exactly** 3, **Exactly** 6 |
| Rewards | **Set price** $30 and $54, **Applies per** **Total** |

**You customize** the grid, the progress message, and how many products load at a time. Items from the whole collection count together toward each tier.

## 4. Buy 3, get 1 free

**The shopper sees** "Buy 3, get 1 free" on a product page. With 4 in the bundle, the cheapest one shows as free.

| Engine piece | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Product set | The product or a collection, **Any mix** |
| Condition | **At least** 4 |
| Reward | 100% **Percentage discount** on 1 unit after the first 3, cheapest first |

**You customize** the copy and how the free unit is shown. The bundle type forms and the AI set up the "after 3, discount 1" part.

## 5. Tiers, an add-on, a gift, and free shipping

**The shopper sees** a pillow on its product page with three quantity tiers. The top tier adds a free pillowcase and free shipping. Below the tiers, an optional duvet cover is offered at 15% off — but only with the pillow.

| Engine piece | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Product sets | The pillow, the pillowcase, the duvet cover |
| Offer 1 | Tiers for 1, 2, and 4 pillows. The top tier also needs 1 pillowcase |
| Offer 1 rewards | 10% and 20% off the pillows; 100% off the pillowcase; a **Shipping price** of 0 on the top tier |
| Offer 2 | The duvet cover, **Exactly** 1, with a **trigger** on the pillow |
| Offer 2 reward | 15% off the duvet cover |

The two offers are independent: the shopper can take the add-on with any tier, and each offer discounts its own items. The trigger means the duvet cover only gets its discount when a pillow is in the cart — whether it was added in this bundle or earlier.

**You customize** the whole layout, the gift line, and the add-on card.

## Build your own

Start from the example closest to your idea and change what you need — or describe the offer to the Bundle Engine and let it assemble the pieces.
