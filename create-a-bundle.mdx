---
title: "Create a bundle"
description: "Create and publish a Knit bundle in Shopify admin"
---

If Knit is not in **Apps** yet, install it from [https://apps.shopify.com/knit-bundles](https://apps.shopify.com/knit-bundles) (listed as **Knit Bundle Engine**). Do not search the App Store by name.

1. Open **Knit Bundles** in Shopify admin.
2. Create a new bundle and choose a type (volume discount, mix and match, and so on).
3. Add products or collections as selectors.
4. Add condition sets and rewards for each tier.
5. Set the bundle to **Active** and save.

The offer then renders on matching product pages through the theme app extension. For Tapcart, Hydrogen, or a custom cart, use the [Headless Bundles API](/headless-bundles-api).
