In Knit, click **Create bundle**. The **Choose bundle type** page offers two paths:

- **A bundle type card** → **Choose**. A ready form for one shape of offer. Knit builds the logic and the widget from your answers.
- **Build with AI** ("Describe it. The engine builds it."). The Bundle Engine works out the structure, the pricing and discount logic, and the widget. You can also build by hand.

Which bundle type cards you see depends on your store. Cards marked **Coming soon** can't be built yet.

## From a bundle type

The form has the same parts for every type, though the fields differ:

1. **The offer** — the products and the discount. For example, a **Fixed bundle** has **Included products** (at least 2) and a **Discount** with **Discount type**: **Percentage off**, **Fixed discount**, **Set price**, **Free shipping**, or **No discount**.
2. **Display options** — where the bundle shows, for example its **Title** and **Visibility** on product pages.
3. **Settings** — **Name** (only you see it), price display such as **Show price per item**, and variant choices such as **Let customers choose different variants for each item**.
4. **Style** — colors, typography, corner radius, and **Custom CSS**. See [Styling a bundle type](#styling-a-bundle-type).
5. **Content and translations** — the widget's wording in each of your store languages.

A new bundle from a bundle type starts as **Active**. Save to publish it; the sync badge shows **Syncing with Shopify** and then **Synced**.

Placement fields (position, popup, priority) are not in bundle type forms; the bundle uses your store's defaults from **Settings**.

### Styling a bundle type

The **Style** card has two tabs:

- **Global theme** — applies to every bundle that uses it. Changes here restyle all of them.
- **Custom design** — this bundle only. Its values win over the global theme.

Both cover **Color**, **Typography**, **Spacing** (corner radius), and **Custom CSS**.

Wording shared by all bundles of a type ("Add to Cart", "Sold out", "Total") is under **Translate shared texts of all bundles** → **Open editor**.

**Settings → Bundle types → Republish all bundles** rebuilds the storefront data of every bundle made from a bundle type. Your settings and products don't change.

## With the Bundle Engine

**Build with AI** opens **Generate Your Bundle with AI**. Describe the bundle — mention products and collections with the mention menu, or attach an image — and click **Generate bundle**. Or choose **Make it manually here**.

The editor has two tabs and a live **Preview**:

- **AI chat** — ask for changes in plain words ("make tier 2 25% off", "put the price under the title").
- **Form** — edit every piece directly.

A Bundle Engine bundle starts as **Draft**.

### The Form tab

| Card | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| General | **Name** (only you see it) and **Discount label** (shoppers see it next to the discount in cart and checkout) |
| **Products used in logic** | Product sets: **Add product**, **Add variant**, **Add collection**, **Add every product**, each with **Matching items must be** |
| Offers | **Set up an offer for this bundle**, **Add tier**, and for each tier its **Conditions** and **Rewards** |

See [Bundle concepts](/bundle-concepts) for what each piece means.

### Display options

Under **Display options → Product detail page**:

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Show this bundle on product pages** | On by default |
| **Show on** | **All the included product pages**, or **Selected products' pages** |
| **Display as** | **Inline widget** (default) or **Pop-up**. A pop-up opens when the shopper clicks the **Theme add-to-cart button** |
| **Widget position** | **Above** or **Below** (default) the **Theme's add-to-cart button**, or a **Custom element** by CSS selector |
| **Add-to-cart method** | **Using widget button** (default): the widget's own button adds the bundle. **Using theme button**: the theme's add-to-cart button adds the bundle instead of the product |
| **Code** | **JS code** and **CSS code** — the widget. See [Widget](/widget) |
| **Content and translation** | The widget's wording per language. **Translate EN → FR** fills empty fields with AI and keeps text you typed |
| **App block** | A **Display handle** to place the widget with the theme's app block. See [Shopify integration](/shopify-integration#placing-the-widget) |
| **Advanced** | **Display priority** (default 1; lower shows first when several bundles match one product) and **Hide elements** (CSS selectors of theme elements to hide) |

### Bundle as Product

The **Bundle as Product (BAP)** card sells the bundle as its own Shopify product, with its own product page. Turning it on creates a new product in Shopify.

- **Product title** and **Price** are required; **Compare-at price** is optional and must be higher than the price.
- **Product description and media** — or **Edit product on Shopify**.
- The product page has its own **Widget position**, **Add-to-cart method**, and **Code**. With no code of its own, it uses the product page's code.
- Turning it off (**Turn off and delete**) deletes the product from Shopify. Orders are not affected.

The product's status and the bundle's status are one: drafting or archiving the product in Shopify turns the bundle off. Sales channels and markets are set in Shopify. See [Shopify integration](/shopify-integration#bundle-as-product) for how it behaves on the store.

### Subscriptions

In the **Subscriptions** card, turn on **Let customers buy this bundle on a subscription**. The widget then offers the selling plans your subscription app defines for each product.

By default the bundle discount applies to the first subscription payment. Change it in **Settings → Subscription discount**: **First payment only**, **Multiple recurring payments**, or **All recurring payments**.

## Status and publishing

- **Status: Active** — the bundle shows on the store and gets the checkout discount.
- **Status: Draft** — hidden. To preview it on your store, add `?nameless_preview=true` to a product page URL (in the Bundle Engine editor, **View on store** does this for you). Previews don't get the discount.

After saving, a banner says **Bundle is live**, **Bundle is in draft**, or **Bundle needs a UI** (a Bundle Engine bundle with no widget code yet).

If the save fails with a message that the shop's active bundles need more bytes than a discount function can read, your active bundles together are too large for Shopify's discount function. Deactivate a bundle, make one smaller, or name fewer collections.

## Store-wide settings

In Knit **Settings**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Default widget position** | The CSS selector bundles are placed next to by default |
| **Hide elements** | CSS selectors of theme elements every bundle hides |
| **Widget button action** | What shoppers see after the widget's button adds the bundle: **Cart drawer** (default), **Checkout**, **Cart page**, or **Do nothing** |
| **Subscription discount** | Which subscription payments get the bundle discount |
| **Discount combinations** | Whether bundle discounts combine with **Product discounts**, **Order discounts**, and **Shipping discounts** (all on by default) |

For other sales channels, see [Integrations](/integrations).
