Every bundle is built from the same pieces. A bundle type's form builds them for you; the Bundle Engine's **Form** tab edits them directly.

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Product sets** | The products in play. In the editor: **Products used in logic** |
| **Offers** | Independent deals inside the bundle. Each offer is a ladder of **tiers** |
| **Conditions** | What the shopper must buy for a tier to apply |
| **Rewards** | What the shopper gets — on products or on shipping |
| **Widget** | How the bundle looks and behaves on the product page |

## Product sets

| Button | The set is |
| --- | --- |
| **Add product** | One product; its variants are the choices |
| **Add variant** | One specific variant |
| **Add collection** | A collection; its products are the choices |
| **Add every product** | Any product in the store, including the one being viewed |

**Matching items must be** sets what counted items share. Product sets offer **The same variant** or **Any mix**; collection and every-product sets offer all three:

| Option | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The same variant** | Every unit is one variant | 3 × Shampoo 250 ml |
| **The same product** | Every unit is one product, any variant | 3 of one shirt, mixed sizes |
| **Any mix** | Units can mix products and variants | Any 3 items from Basics |

## Offers and tiers

An **offer** is one deal; its **tiers** form a ladder, smallest first (for example, buy 2 → 10% off, buy 4 → 20% off). In each offer the shopper gets **one** tier — the highest their picks meet, or the one they tap in the widget.

A bundle can hold **several offers**. They are independent and can all apply, each on its own units.

## Conditions

A condition is: **Customer buys** a number of items **counted from** one or more product sets.

| **Customer buys** | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **At least** | This number or more |
| **At most** | This number or fewer |
| **Exactly** | This number only |
| **Between** | An inclusive range |

- **Selected sets count together.** "Between 2 and 5 from Shampoo and Conditioner" is met by 1 shampoo + 2 conditioners. To require each, add one condition per set.
- **A maximum caps; it doesn't refuse.** With "at most 5", a shopper who buys 7 gets the discount on 5.
- **+ AND new condition** — all must hold. **+ OR** — any one is enough.
- **Apply to every tier** (badge **All tiers**) — every tier of the offer must meet the condition.

### Triggers

A **trigger** counts matching items **anywhere in the cart** and uses none of them — "the shopper already has X". Each item qualifies an offer once per cart. In the Form tab, tick **Only unlocks the offer: these items stay full price** on an **At least** condition.

## Rewards

A reward applies to the product sets under **Discount applies to** — not necessarily the set the condition counts ("buy the phone, get 20% off a case").

| **Reward type** | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Percentage discount** | **Percentage** | A percent off |
| **Fixed discount** | **Amount** | An amount off, in your shop currency |
| **Set price** | **Price** | What the items end up costing. `0` makes them free |

For a fixed discount or set price, **Applies per** says how the value spreads:

| **Applies per** | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Total** | $10 off on two sets is $10 off their combined subtotal |
| **Per selector** | $10 off on two sets is $20 off |
| **Per unit** | $5 off with quantity 3 is $15 off |

A tier can have several rewards; they all apply.

### Buy X, get Y

A reward can skip the first X units, then discount up to Y, cheapest first. "Buy 3, get 1 free" is 100% off, skip 3, discount 1. In the Form tab, tick **Discount only the cheapest items** and set **Number of items to discount**. The skip is set by bundle types and the AI.

### Shipping rewards

**+ Add shipping reward** (one per tier):

| **Shipping reward** | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Shipping price** | The shopper pays this for shipping. `0` is free shipping |
| **Fixed discount** | An amount off shipping, at most the shipping cost |
| **Percentage discount** | A percent off shipping |

## Widget

A bundle type ships a ready widget you style in its **Style** panel. A Bundle Engine bundle has its own **JS code** and **CSS code**. See [Widget](/widget).

The discount itself is applied at checkout — see [Shopify integration](/shopify-integration#checkout). For full examples, see [What you can build](/what-you-can-build).
