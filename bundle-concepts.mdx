title: "Bundle concepts"
description: "How Knit models products, conditions, and rewards"
---

A Knit bundle is a merchandising offer: which products participate, when the offer applies, and what discount the shopper gets.

In the editor, products live in **Product sets**. Each set has an **Included item** type. There are **three**.

## Selectors (product sets)

### 1. Single Product

You pick **one product**.

Use this when the offer is that exact item — for example “buy 2 of this shampoo, save 15%.”

- Click **Select** and choose the product.
- The shopper sees that product (variant + quantity).
- The widget can show on **that product’s page**.

### 2. Single Product (Collection-backed)

You pick a **collection**, not one product.

Use this when the same offer should run on every product in a collection, without adding each product by hand — for example “buy 2 of whichever t-shirt they are looking at in Basics.”

- Click **Select** and choose a collection.
- On a product page, Knit uses **the product the shopper is viewing**, but only if it is in that collection.
- The shopper is still buying **that one product** (plus quantity). They are not picking several products from the collection.
- If the page product is not in the collection, this set does not apply on that page.

### 3. Shopify Collection

You pick a **collection**. The shopper can choose **more than one product** from it.

Use this for mix and match, build-a-box, or “pick any 3 from this collection.”

- Click **Select** and choose a collection.
- The widget lists products from the collection. Quantity on each row starts at 0 until the shopper adds it.
- Quantity and discount rules apply across those picks.

### How to choose

| You want | Use |
| --- | --- |
| One specific product | **Single Product** |
| Same volume offer on every product in a collection, one product per page | **Single Product (Collection-backed)** |
| Shopper mixes several products from a collection | **Shopify Collection** |

You can combine sets. Example: **Single Product** for a hero item, plus **Shopify Collection** for add-ons.

## Conditions and rewards

After product sets, you define **when** the offer applies and **what** the shopper gets.

Each **condition-reward** pair is one tier. Knit checks the conditions on that card; if they are met, those rewards apply — for example “Buy 2, save 19%”. Use **Add new condition-reward** for the next tier, such as “Buy 4, save 25%”.

### Conditions

A condition is a quantity rule: **Customer buys** this many **items from** a product set.

There are **four** comparisons:

| Comparison | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **at least** | This number or more | Buy 2 or more |
| **at most** | This number or fewer | Buy no more than 5 |
| **exactly** | This number only | Buy exactly 2 |
| **between** | An inclusive range | Buy 2–4 |

Pick which **product set** the count applies to:

- **Single Product** and **Single Product (Collection-backed)** — units of that one product.
- **Shopify Collection** — total units the shopper picked from the collection.

You can add more than one condition on the same card. All of them must be true for the reward to apply — for example “at least 1 from the hero set” and “at least 2 from add-ons”.

### Rewards

A reward is the discount: **Customer gets** this **discount on** a product set.

**Percentage** — a percent off those items (0–100). Example: 19% off.

**Fixed amount** — a money amount off, in your shop currency. Then choose how it spreads:

| Mode | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Total** | The amount off the qualifying items as a group, once. $10 on two product sets is $10 off the combined subtotal. |
| **Per selector** | The amount off each product set you attached the reward to. $10 on two product sets is $20 off. |
| **Per unit** | The amount off each unit in those product sets. $5 with quantity 3 is $15 off. |

The product set on the reward can be the same as the condition, or a different one — for example, require a quantity of product A and discount product B.

The discount is applied at Shopify checkout, not as a precomputed product price. See [How discounts apply](/how-discounts-apply).

## Bundle state

Only **Active** bundles appear on the storefront.
