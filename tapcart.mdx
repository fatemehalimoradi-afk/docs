---
title: "Tapcart"
description: "Add the Fast Bundle 2.0 widget to a Tapcart product page"
---

Fast Bundle 2.0 publishes a block to Tapcart App Studio; you add it to the product page, no code needed. It shows your store's bundles, adds to **Tapcart's native cart**, and the discount applies in Shopify checkout.

## Prerequisites

- Fast Bundle 2.0 installed and opened once in Shopify admin (see [Getting started](/getting-started))
- At least one **Active** bundle
- Tapcart **Enterprise** with [App Studio](https://studio.tapcart.com) access
- The **Fast Bundle 2.0** block published to your app (App Studio → **My Blocks**). If it's missing, contact your Fast Bundle 2.0 account manager.

## Add the block

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place it on the product page">
    In App Studio, open the **Product Detail Page** template, click **Add Block**, choose **Fast Bundle 2.0**, and drag it into place — typically above the native Add to Cart button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional settings">
    Usually leave these as pre-filled.

    | Setting | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | App origin override | **Leave blank** (production) unless Fast Bundle 2.0 support asks. A wrong value leaves the widget blank |
    | Bundle handle, Bundle type | **Leave blank.** They don't limit which bundles show |
    | Initial height in px | Height on the static App Studio canvas, and for products with no bundle. Default `600`. On device the widget sizes to content |
    | Debug mode | `true` or `1` shows console logs and a diagnostic panel. **Shoppers see it too — leave blank in production** |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Save the screen, then **Publish** the app (or push a preview build).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify

The App Studio canvas is static. Test in **Preview your app** or on a device: open a product with an **Active** bundle, tap the bundle **Add to Cart**, and check the Tapcart cart shows every bundle line. A test order should show `__FB_ATC_UID` and the discount on each line in **Orders**.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blank widget | No Active bundle for this product, or app never opened | Add the product to an **Active** bundle; open Fast Bundle 2.0 once in Shopify admin |
| Console: refused to connect | Tapcart WebView blocking the widget origin | Contact Fast Bundle 2.0 support |
| Fast Bundle 2.0 not in My Blocks | Block not published to this app | Contact your Fast Bundle 2.0 account manager |
| Items in cart, no discount | Automatic discount inactive, or property stripped | See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#discount-missing-at-checkout) |

Bundle changes can take up to about **6 minutes** to show in the app.

<Note>
  Product page only; the block doesn't replace Tapcart's Add to Cart button. In the app, bundles are one-time purchase only, pop-up and hidden bundles don't appear, **Show on → Selected products' pages** isn't applied, and the widget uses your store's default language and market prices.
</Note>
